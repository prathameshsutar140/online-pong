Left paddle (Player 1) controlled by mouse. In Single-player, arrow keys also move left paddle. Local 2-player uses W/S for left and Up/Down for right.

After clicking, wait a moment for ICE gathering then copy the Offer text and send to your friend.
Paste Answer back here (from your friend) then click Apply Answer
Join: paste Host's offer, create answer and send it back
Paste the Offer you received and click Create Answer. Copy the Answer text and send back to the Host.
Controls summary: Single: left paddle = mouse (or ArrowUp/ArrowDown). Local 2-player: left = W/S or mouse, right = ArrowUp/ArrowDown. Online: Host is left; Guest is right and uses Up/Down arrows (or mouse on host). Use the Signaling boxes to exchange Offer/Answer manually (copy-paste).
